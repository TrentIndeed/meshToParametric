April 2026

Feature AI Customer Service Bot

Live AI support widget on every page. Ask questions about features, pricing, or get help with your meshes.

April 2026

Feature Token-Based Pricing

Pay for complexity, not flat fees. Simple parts cost ~20 tokens, complex parts ~55. Free tier gets 100 tokens.

April 2026

Feature Job Queue for Concurrent Users

Multiple users can upload simultaneously. Each job gets its own ID, progress bar, and isolated state. No part data is stored on our servers.

April 2026

UI Two-Step Upload Flow

Upload shows the raw mesh first with complexity estimate and token cost. Click "Analyze & Build" to run the pipeline.

March 2026

Feature Chamfer & Fillet Detection

Stage 5 now recognizes cone surfaces as chamfers and partial-arc cylinders as fillets. 10 chamfers detected on the BottomMount test part.

March 2026

Feature CadQuery Solid Reconstruction

Full sketch → extrude → cut holes → chamfer pipeline builds clean B-rep geometry from scratch. Construction slideshow GIF with 3 camera angles.

March 2026

Feature 6-Stage Pipeline Complete

All stages implemented: Ingest → Segment → Fit → Topology → Recognize → Export. Tested on 5 mesh models.

March 2026

UI Three.js Web Viewer

Interactive 3D CAD viewer with feature tree, AI chatbot, construction timeline with rendered thumbnails, and Fusion 360-style PBR rendering.
